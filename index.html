        About this tool: Clinical references, architecture & development methodology

        Clinical Architecture & Intent: Conceived, clinically mapped, and authored by an active practicing neurologist. This tool serves as a rapid pre-referral checklist and documentation generator to screen potential candidates for monoclonal anti-amyloid therapies (e.g., lecanemab, donanemab) prior to subspecialty evaluation. It operates entirely client-side without collecting, processing, or transmitting Protected Health Information (PHI).

        Development Methodology: Developed via a rigorous physician-in-the-loop, AI-assisted context engineering workflow. Decision logic, safety cutoff thresholds, clinical phrasing, and risk strata were physician-specified and iteratively validated against established clinical practice guidelines, with large language model assistance leveraged solely for front-end interface synthesis and code generation.

        Primary Clinical Sources & References:

        • Donanemab Appropriate Use Recommendations: Cummings J, et al. Donanemab: Appropriate Use Recommendations. J Prev Alzheimers Dis. 2025; 12(1):100094.
        • Lecanemab Appropriate Use Recommendations: Cummings J, et al. Lecanemab: Appropriate Use Recommendations. J Prev Alzheimers Dis. 2023; 10(3):362-377.
        • Amyloid-Related Imaging Abnormalities (ARIA) & ApoE Risk: Reiman EM, et al. / Phase 3 Clarity AD & TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 safety monitoring data on ApoE ε4 carrier stratification and microhemorrhage thresholds.
        • Plasma Biomarker Guidance: Alzheimer's Association Workgroup on Diagnostic Criteria and Plasma Biomarkers (pTau-217 renal clearance threshold / eGFR validity considerations).

        Disclaimer: This tool is intended for licensed medical providers (MD/DO, PA, NP) as an informal clinical decision aid and documentation assistant. It does not substitute for independent clinical judgment, formal specialty cognitive neurology consultation, or manufacturer prescribing information.
